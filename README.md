# Blood in the hall
## what is it?

Hi. This is a murder mystery created on google slides. It's called "Blood in the hall". It's about a case of death of a young student. There are 3 suspects - The teacher, friend and a neighbor. The player have to look into the evidences and decide who the real killer is.

## How to play?
1. Download the pptx file from this repo.
2. Upload it to google slides and click on slideshow
3. Don't open it using powerpoint. It may break some hyperlinks and animations.

**OR**

Directly open it on [google slides](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/1Sed8tRldTcLZURrVlhOA8fqkuHIaPBNIGUh64gcF5iQ/edit?usp=sharing)
